Disk Scheduling Algorithms

SCAN and C-SCAN

🔄 SCAN Algorithm

SCAN is also called the elevator algorithm. The disk head moves in one direction and services all requests encountered along the path. After reaching the end of the disk, the direction is reversed.

  1. Start from the current head position.
  2. Move in the selected direction.
  3. Service requests encountered along the way.
  4. Reach the end of the disk.
  5. Reverse direction and continue servicing requests.

↔️ C-SCAN Algorithm

C-SCAN stands for Circular SCAN. The disk head moves in only one direction. After reaching one end, it immediately moves to the opposite end and continues servicing requests.

  1. Start from the current head position.
  2. Move in one direction.
  3. Service requests along the path.
  4. Reach the end of the disk.
  5. Jump to the opposite end.
  6. Continue servicing requests in the same direction.

SCAN vs C-SCAN

Feature SCAN C-SCAN
Movement Both directions One direction
Direction Change Yes No
Fairness Good Very Good
Implementation Simple Slightly complex
