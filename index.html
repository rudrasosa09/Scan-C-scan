Disk Scheduling Algorithms

Explore important disk scheduling techniques

🔄

SCAN Algorithm

The SCAN algorithm moves the disk head in one direction, servicing requests along the way. When it reaches the end of the disk, it reverses direction.

↔️

C-SCAN Algorithm

C-SCAN moves the disk head in one direction only. After reaching the end, it returns to the beginning without servicing requests during the return.
